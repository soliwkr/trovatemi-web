# TROVATEMI PUBLIC EXPERIENCE v3

Status: **CURRENT — founder direction 2026-09-11**

This supersedes `trovatemi-public-v2.md`.

## Direction

Trovatemi is **campaign-grade, adult, popular, direct, visual, local and credible**.
It must feel like one brand across website, Stories/Reels, ads, sales material and physical QR/NFC objects.

## Core idea

> **Il passaparola che hai già. Messo al lavoro.**

Trovatemi takes what already happens inside a good local business — happy customers, compliments, repeat visits — and makes more of that value visible outside the business.

Core bridge:

```
customer satisfaction
→ public proof
→ reply / care
→ reusable proof
→ more confidence at the moment of choice
```

## Master tension

Every strong Trovatemi communication makes this tension visible:

```
REALITY                     PERCEPTION
you may be better           they may look safer
happy customers             little public proof
compliments in the shop     silence online
good work                   weak visible evidence
```

## Campaign territories

1. **Peccato che Google non era lì.** — customer delight disappears after the visit.
2. **Sei più bravo. Lui ha 384 recensioni. Tu 41.** — quality vs visible confidence.
3. **50 clienti felici. 4 recensioni.** — customer volume vs public proof.
4. **Le persone lo dicono. Noi lo facciamo lavorare.** — product/mechanism reveal.
5. **FORMIA / territory campaigns.** — Le persone ti cercano. Fatti trovare.

## Visual grammar

- Black `#08090A`, white, warm paper `#F5F3ED`, Trovatemi yellow `#F2BD18`.
- Heavy condensed display typography; clean grotesk body.
- Handwriting only as a human-proof layer: quote, receipt, chalkboard, annotation.
- Prefer cinematic real-world local-business photography, owners, clients, storefronts, phone-in-hand choice moments and physical QR/NFC objects.
- Allow hard black panels, yellow highlights, receipts, chalkboards and phone frames when they carry evidence.
- Avoid SaaS card soup, decorative dashboards, fake metrics and fake social proof.

## Proof demonstration

The public experience is a **Trovatemi demonstration**, not an audit and not a quiz.
The internal route may remain `/beauty-check/` during the first Beauty campaign.

Sequence:

```
search your business
→ see your public proof
→ see one defensible same-query alternative
→ declare weekly client volume + recent review volume
→ see the passaparola contrast
→ answer 3 breakpoint questions together
→ receive one explainable diagnosis
→ see 3 actions
→ see Trovatemi as the system that closes the loop
```

Three breakpoints only: **collect, reply, reuse**. No master score.

## Quantification rules

- Weekly clients are owner-declared unless a reliable source exists.
- Recent reviews are owner-declared unless a reliable measured source exists.
- Monthly experiences may be estimated as weekly × 4.33.
- Always state that not every customer would leave a review.
- Never label the arithmetic difference as lost reviews.

## Evidence rules

Allowed: Google provider truth, selected-place details, same-query context described exactly as context, owner-declared process/volumes, deterministic diagnosis, verified product capability.

Never: invented ranking, invented benchmark, public 0–100 score, unsupported causality, review gating, guaranteed ranking, fake review velocity or fake activation.

## Offer truth

- one product: Trovatemi;
- Beauty & Wellness is the first commercial lens, not the master brand;
- 21 days at €0;
- then €149/month per location;
- payment method at activation when real activation exists;
- cancel before renewal;
- no review gating.

Until real checkout exists, the preview CTA must route to a truthful offer/contact step rather than simulate activation.

## Acceptance

A screen passes only if the owner understands the idea in seconds, one idea dominates, the visual supports the claim, the strongest claim is defensible, and the screen feels like the same Trovatemi brand as the campaign materials without collapsing into generic SaaS aesthetics.
